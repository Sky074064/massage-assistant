🖐 Privacy Policy

Massage Assistant — Auto Fill Notes
Last updated: April 2026

Summary: This extension does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. All data stays on your device or is used solely to verify your license key.

1. Who We Are

Massage Assistant is a Chrome Extension developed independently for massage therapists using mybookrelax.com. Contact: sky074064@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to operate the extension:

  • License key — entered voluntarily by the user to unlock paid features. Sent to our license verification server to confirm validity.
  • Referral code — a randomly generated 8-character ID created locally on your device when you install the extension. Used to track referral rewards. This code does not identify you personally.
  • Note usage count — the number of treatment notes filled. Stored locally in Chrome's sync storage only.
  • Template content — treatment note templates (A–E slots) you type yourself. Stored locally in Chrome's sync storage only.
  • Email address — only if you voluntarily provide it on our landing page to purchase a license. Sent to our payment processor (Gumroad) and our license delivery system (n8n) solely to send your license key.

3. What Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Client names, dates, or any patient information shown on mybookrelax.com
  • Treatment note content you fill in
  • Browsing history or activity on any other website
  • Any data from pages other than mybookrelax.com

4. How We Use Your Data

  • License key: Verified against our server to confirm your subscription is active. The key and your email (as the owner field) are stored in our license server to validate future requests.
  • Referral code: Stored on our server (n8n + Google Sheets) to track whether a referred user has purchased, so we can send the referrer a reward. No personal identity is attached to the code.
  • Email (purchase only): Used exclusively to deliver your license key. Not used for marketing without consent.

5. Data Storage

  • Local (Chrome sync storage): Templates, settings, note count, license key, referral code. Synced across your Chrome devices via Google's infrastructure.
  • Our server (n8n cloud): License keys and email addresses for license verification. Referral code to email mapping for reward tracking.
  • Google Sheets: Purchase records (email, plan, referral code, timestamp) for business operations.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Gumroad — payment processor. Governed by Gumroad's Privacy Policy.
  • n8n Cloud — workflow automation used for license delivery. Data processed within their platform.
  • Google Sheets / Google Drive — used for business record keeping. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. User Consent

By installing this extension and entering your license key or email, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. You may request deletion of your data by emailing sky074064@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention

License and purchase records are retained for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.
